Kundli, also known as Janam Kundli or birth chart, is one of the most important concepts in Vedic astrology. It represents the positions of planets and celestial bodies at the exact time, date, and place of a person’s birth. By studying these planetary positions, astrologers interpret different aspects of life, including personality, career, marriage, finances, health, family relationships, and important life events.

A Kundli is often considered a cosmic blueprint of an individual’s life. It can help a person understand their natural strengths, challenges, opportunities, and tendencies. When interpreted properly, it may provide valuable guidance for making thoughtful decisions and preparing for different phases of life.

## What Is a Kundli?

A Kundli is created using three essential details: date of birth, exact time of birth, and place of birth. These details determine the positions of the Sun, Moon, planets, zodiac signs, and twelve houses. Each house represents a particular area of life, such as personality, wealth, communication, family, education, career, marriage, children, health, and spirituality.

## Kundli and Career

The tenth house is particularly significant for career, profession, and reputation. Astrologers analyze its ruling planet and aspects to understand professional tendencies, whether in business, employment, administration, technology, education, or creative fields.

## Kundli for Marriage and Relationships

Marriage matching, or **[Guna Milan](ca://s?q=Guna_Milan_in_Kundli)**, compares two birth charts to assess compatibility. The seventh house, planetary positions, and doshas are examined to highlight harmony and challenges.

## Understanding Doshas in Kundli

Certain planetary combinations create doshas, such as **[Mangal Dosha](ca://s?q=Mangal_Dosha_in_Kundli)** or **[Kaal Sarp Dosha](ca://s?q=Kaal_Sarp_Dosha_in_Kundli)**. Remedies may include prayers, mantras, charitable activities, or pujas, depending on the chart.

## Future Planning with Kundli

Astrology uses **[Mahadasha](ca://s?q=Mahadasha_in_Kundli)** and **[Antardasha](ca://s?q=Antardasha_in_Kundli)** to interpret life phases. These highlight opportunities and challenges, encouraging reflection and planning.

## Importance of Accurate Birth Details

Precise birth time, date, and place are crucial for accuracy. Even small differences can affect ascendant and house placements. When exact time is unavailable, astrologers may use birth-time rectification methods.
